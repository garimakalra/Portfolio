About

Designer for the systems that don't fit on one screen.

I'm a Staff UX/UI designer who works on enterprise products where the complexity is real and the stakes are high — infrastructure monitoring, cloud monetization, fleet compliance. These aren't consumer apps you can charm with polish; they're dense systems where a confused user means a missed outage, a billing error, or a compliance risk.

My work is making that complexity legible. That means information architecture that matches how people actually think, interaction patterns that hold up under pressure, and — increasingly — designing how AI fits into expert workflows without eroding trust. Just as often it means the less visible work: framing the ambiguous problem, arguing for the right sequence of work, and keeping a team honest about outcomes over output.

I came to design through QA engineering — which means I think in system behavior and edge cases, and I collaborate with engineers on what's actually feasible rather than lobbing over ideal-world mockups. A decade across three enterprise domains has taught me to get fluent in an unfamiliar system fast and find the leverage point that makes the rest tractable.
